Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

DoWeo is an expense-sharing and personal finance tracking application. This policy explains the information the service may process, why it is needed, and your choices. It applies to DoWeo's website and related mobile experience.

1. Information you provide

When you create an account or use DoWeo, you may provide your email address, username, display name, profile photo, biography, payment handles, tracker membership, expense descriptions, amounts, splits, settlements, comments, messages, feedback, and attachments. You may also provide information about other participants in a shared tracker.

2. Technical information

The service may process sign-in events, device and browser information, diagnostic records, and security-related metadata needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect accounts. We do not claim to collect every category listed on every visit.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, operate trackers and expense sharing, display balances and activity, enable communications and notifications, respond to support requests, improve reliability, and prevent misuse. We do not represent DoWeo as a bank or payment processor.

4. Shared information

Information entered in shared trackers, including relevant names, expenses, comments, balances, and settlement activity, may be visible to other authorized members of those trackers. Avoid posting sensitive information that other members should not see.

5. Service providers

DoWeo uses third-party infrastructure for functions such as authentication, database storage, hosting, and email delivery. These providers may process information on DoWeo's behalf as needed to deliver their services. The specific provider list and any additional integrations should be verified against the production configuration before publication.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and tracker data may be retained while needed to provide the service, maintain shared records, resolve disputes, comply with applicable obligations, and maintain security. Deleting an account may not immediately erase records that remain relevant to other participants' shared trackers or limited backup copies. To request information about deletion, contact help.doweo@gmail.com.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your login credentials and report suspected unauthorized activity.

8. Your choices

You may be able to edit profile details, change notification preferences, leave trackers, and request account deletion through available app controls. Contact us for help accessing, correcting, or deleting personal information, subject to applicable law and the rights of other users.

9. Children and teens

DoWeo should not be used by anyone who does not meet the minimum age requirements stated in the Terms of Service. If you believe a child's information has been provided inappropriately, contact us.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our practices change. Material updates should be communicated appropriately, and the effective date will be revised. Privacy questions may be sent to help.doweo@gmail.com.
