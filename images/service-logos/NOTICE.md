# Service logos

Desktop service logos are stored locally as SVG so display quality does not depend on small remote favicons.

- OpenAI, Google and Claude: @lobehub/icons-static-svg 1.103.0 (MIT), from https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons. License included in LOBEHUB-LICENSE.txt.
- Netflix, Max, Apple TV, Spotify, YouTube and Dropbox: simple-icons 16.38.0 (CC0-1.0), https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons.
- Amazon Prime: simple-icons 11.15.0 (CC0-1.0).

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners. SVG dimensions and monochrome colors are normalized for the existing service icon surfaces.
