Privacy Policy

Draft for review. This document has not been finalized or verified against all service practices. It should not be represented as a finalized legal agreement or policy.

DoWeo is a shared-expense tracking website. This notice describes how information may be handled when you use the service. This draft must be checked against DoWeo's actual backend, retention settings, and third-party services before publication.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, DoWeo may handle your email address, account and display names, username, profile image, payment-app usernames, tracker membership, expense descriptions and amounts, split allocations, payment records you enter, messages, receipt images, and other uploaded content.

How it is used and shared

Information is used to operate accounts, calculate and display shared balances, manage trackers, display messages and activity, and provide requested features. Members of a shared tracker may see information relevant to that tracker, including expenses, balances, member profiles, and chat content. Do not add sensitive information you do not want other tracker members to see.

Service providers and storage

DoWeo uses Supabase for authentication and application data and Cloudflare Pages to host its website. These providers may process information needed to deliver the service. The site may also use browser storage to keep preferences or session-related information. Any additional analytics, email, image-processing, or payment providers should be identified here before publication if they are used.

Security and retention

DoWeo aims to restrict access to account and tracker information, but no online service can guarantee complete security. Information may remain in account records, shared tracker history, backups, or logs for different periods. Specific retention and deletion periods have not yet been verified.

Your choices

You can update certain profile information in Settings. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of your information, email the address below. Removing an account may not automatically erase shared records that other tracker members rely on; the actual deletion process and any legal obligations should be verified.

Children and location

DoWeo's minimum permitted user age and rules for minors need to be established before this policy is published. Privacy rights and consent requirements may vary by location.

Updates

This policy may be updated when DoWeo's features or data practices change. Material changes should be communicated appropriately.
